# Explanatory cutaway concept / Concepto de vista en corte

## English

```text
Create a conceptual cutaway of [object] showing [components in their order] based on [reference]. Use simple material colors, keep shared alignment between components, and reserve open space for labels.
```

## Spanish

```text
Crea una vista en corte conceptual de [objeto] que muestre [componentes en orden] a partir de [referencia]. Usa colores de material sencillos, conserva la alineación entre componentes y reserva espacio para etiquetas.
```
