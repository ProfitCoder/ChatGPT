# Restore a printed photograph / Restaurar una fotografía impresa

## English

```text
Restore [scanned print] by repairing dust spots, fold marks, and torn edges. Retain the original grain and tonal range. Reconstruct damaged areas conservatively, especially faces and lettering.
```

## Spanish

```text
Restaura [copia escaneada] reparando manchas de polvo, dobleces y bordes rotos. Conserva el grano y la gama tonal originales. Reconstruye las zonas dañadas con prudencia, especialmente caras y letras.
```
