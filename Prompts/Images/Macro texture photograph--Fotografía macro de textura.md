# Macro texture photograph / Fotografía macro de textura

## English

```text
Photograph [material] at macro scale with raking light to reveal surface relief. Place the sharpest focus on [detail] and let the remaining texture fall gently out of focus.
```

## Spanish

```text
Fotografía [material] a escala macro con luz rasante para revelar el relieve. Sitúa el foco más nítido en [detalle] y desenfoca gradualmente el resto de la textura.
```
